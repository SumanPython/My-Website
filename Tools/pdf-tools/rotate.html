Rotate PDF Pages Online for Free

Correct the orientation of your PDF documents with the free online PDF rotator from AllInOne. Whether a scanned document contains sideways pages, upside-down layouts, or mixed orientations, you can quickly adjust page direction without installing any software.

How to Rotate a PDF

  • Upload or drag and drop your PDF file into the drop zone.
  • Select your preferred rotation angle: 90°, 180°, or 270°.
  • Choose whether to rotate all pages or only selected pages using the checkboxes.
  • Click Download Rotated PDF to save your corrected document instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. All processing happens locally inside your browser memory using WebAssembly and JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to any external server.

Can I rotate individual pages instead of the whole document?

Yes! You can rotate individual pages separately using each page's rotation button or apply angles selectively.
