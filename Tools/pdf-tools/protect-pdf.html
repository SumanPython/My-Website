Protect PDF Documents with a Password Online

Protect PDF is a simple way to add a layer of privacy to documents that contain information you do not want everyone to open. Whether you are sharing an invoice, business proposal, personal letter, application, or report, adding an opening password can help keep the contents private. This tool lets you set a password for your PDF in just a few steps without installing desktop software.

Select a PDF from your device and enter a password that you can remember but is difficult for others to guess. Confirm the password to avoid typing mistakes, then choose whether to allow printing, copying, or editing in PDF readers that respect document permissions. When you protect the file, the tool creates an encrypted copy for download and leaves your original file untouched.

How to Password Protect a PDF

  • Choose or drag your PDF file into the upload area.
  • Enter a strong opening password and confirm it.
  • Select the document permissions you want to allow.
  • Click Protect PDF and wait for the encryption process to finish.
  • Download the protected copy and keep the password in a safe place.

Keep Your Documents Private

Processing happens in your browser, so your document is not uploaded to our website. Use a unique password rather than a password you use for other accounts. For sensitive files, share the password with intended recipients through a separate communication channel. Remember that password protection can make a file difficult or impossible to open if you lose the password, so save it securely before distributing the document.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I protect a PDF with a password?

Upload your PDF, enter a password and confirm it, choose any permission preferences, then press Protect PDF. Download the encrypted copy once it is ready.

Does the tool upload my file?

No. PDF processing runs in your browser. The selected document is not sent to OnlineToolCollection servers.

Will it change my original PDF?

No. The tool creates a separate protected PDF for you to download. Your original file is not modified.

What happens if I forget my password?

Without the correct password, opening an encrypted PDF may not be possible. Keep your password safely stored before sharing or deleting the original.
