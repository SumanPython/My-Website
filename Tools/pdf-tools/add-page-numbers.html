Add Page Numbers to PDF Online — Fast, Free & Private

Whether you are submitting a university thesis, preparing legal court exhibits, compiling business reports, or organizing scanned receipts, clearly numbered pages make any document easier to read and reference. Our Add Page Numbers to PDF tool lets you stamp crisp, properly aligned page numbers onto every page of your PDF in seconds—without installing Adobe Acrobat or uploading sensitive files to external servers.

Everything runs 100% client-side inside your web browser. Your PDF never leaves your computer or phone, ensuring total privacy for confidential contracts, academic drafts, and personal records.

Customizable Numbering Options

  • Six Flexible Positions: Place numbers at the Bottom Center, Bottom Right, Bottom Left, Top Center, Top Right, or Top Left with clean, balanced page margins.
  • Multiple Numbering Styles: Choose simple numbers (1), fraction style (1 / 10), formal pagination (Page 1 of 10), or decorative dashes (- 1 -).
  • Skip Cover Pages Easily: Set Start Numbering From Page to 2 (or any page) so your title page or table of contents remains unnumbered.
  • Custom Font Size & Color: Match your document's typography by adjusting the point size and picking any hex color.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I start numbering from page 2 to keep my cover page blank?

Yes! Set Start Numbering From Page to 2 and keep First Page Number Value at 1. Page 1 will stay untouched, and Page 2 will be labeled as Page 1.

2. Does this work on scanned PDFs or single-page PDFs?

Yes. It works on 100% of standard and scanned PDFs regardless of page count or internal structure.

3. Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Never. All page numbering happens locally in your browser's memory, so your documents stay completely private on your device.
