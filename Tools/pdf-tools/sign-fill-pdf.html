Sign and Fill PDF Documents Online for Free

Need to sign a contract, complete a form, or add a short note to a PDF without printing it? This free Sign & Fill PDF tool lets you place a handwritten signature or text directly onto your document. Open your PDF in the browser, choose a placement mode, and click or tap the page to add your content. It is designed for quick document updates on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

For privacy, your PDF is processed locally in your browser. The document is not uploaded to our website, so you can edit it without sending it to a server. You control where the signature or text appears and can download a new PDF when you are ready. Review the document carefully before sharing it, especially if it contains official, legal, or financial information.

Key Features

  • Draw a signature: Use a mouse, stylus, or touchscreen to draw a signature on the signature pad.
  • Add text to PDF: Type names, dates, short notes, or form responses and place them on the page.
  • Page navigation: Move through multi-page PDFs and place items on the page you need.
  • Private processing: The editing process runs in your browser, with no PDF upload to our server.
  • Mobile-friendly controls: Responsive layout and touch-enabled signature drawing make it usable on smaller screens.

How to Sign or Fill a PDF

Choose a PDF from your device to load the preview. Draw your signature in the signature area, select Add Signature, then click or tap the page to place it. To fill a form, switch to Add Text, enter your text, adjust its size, and click the desired location. Use the page controls to edit other pages. When you finish, select Download Signed / Filled PDF to save your updated document. The original file remains unchanged.

Use It for Common PDF Tasks

This browser-based PDF signer can help with everyday documents such as application forms, acknowledgement pages, simple agreements, school paperwork, and business forms that need a signature or a few typed entries. It is intended for basic annotation-style signing and filling. It does not validate the legal identity of the signer or create a certificate-based digital signature.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I sign a PDF online?

Upload your PDF, draw your signature in the signature area, choose Add Signature, and click or tap the document to place it. Download your updated PDF when done.

2. Can I type text into a PDF?

Yes. Select Add Text, enter your text, set the font size, then click the page to place it. Repeat to add multiple text entries.

3. Are my documents uploaded or stored?

No. The PDF is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to our website. Download the updated file to keep your copy.

4. Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. The page is responsive, and the signature pad accepts touch input. Tap the PDF preview to place signature or text items.

5. Does this create a certified digital signature?

No. It adds a visible signature image or text to the PDF. It does not create cryptographic signing, identity verification, or certificate validation.
