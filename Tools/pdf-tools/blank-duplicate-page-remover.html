Frequently Asked Questions

How does the blank page detector work?

The tool renders each page and checks the amount of visible content. Pages that are nearly empty are flagged as potential blank pages for review.

How are duplicate PDF pages detected?

Rendered page images are compared for visual similarity. Pages that look very similar are flagged as possible duplicates. You should review the previews before deleting them.

Will it delete pages automatically?

No. Detected pages are presented for review. You choose which pages to remove before creating the cleaned PDF.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No upload is required. The PDF is processed locally in your browser using JavaScript libraries.

Can I use this on mobile?

Yes. The page is responsive and supports mobile browsers, tablets, and desktop devices.

Remove Blank and Duplicate Pages from PDF Files Online

Unwanted blank pages and repeated content can make a PDF document difficult to read, print, share, or organize. Whether you are working with scanned paperwork, reports, study materials, invoices, manuals, or business documents, removing unnecessary pages can make your file cleaner and easier to manage. Our free blank and duplicate PDF page remover helps you identify pages that may not need to be included in your document.

Instead of manually opening every page of a large PDF, use this tool to scan the document and identify nearly empty pages and visually repeated pages. After the scan is complete, review the page previews, select the pages you want to delete, and download a new PDF containing the remaining pages.

Find Blank PDF Pages Quickly

Blank pages can appear when scanning books, printing double-sided documents, combining files, or exporting documents from other applications. Our blank page detection feature checks the visible content of rendered PDF pages and flags pages that appear to be empty. This can help you clean up unnecessary pages in scanned documents and reduce the number of pages you need to print. Very faint marks, small page numbers, or light content may affect automatic detection, so reviewing flagged pages is recommended.

Detect Repeated PDF Pages

Duplicate pages can occur when documents are scanned more than once, files are merged incorrectly, or the same page is accidentally included in multiple sections. The duplicate detection feature compares page appearance and identifies pages that look similar. It can help with repeated forms, reports, handouts, and other documents. Since pages with similar layouts may contain different information, check the previews before deciding which pages to remove.
