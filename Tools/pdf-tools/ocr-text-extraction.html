Free Online OCR Text Extraction — Convert Scanned PDFs & Images to Text

Have a scanned PDF contract, photographed receipt, book page, or screenshot where you cannot highlight or copy the words? Our OCR Text Extraction tool uses Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to read text inside images and scanned documents and turn it into clean, editable, searchable text in seconds.

Everything runs 100% client-side inside your web browser using WebAssembly. Your sensitive invoices, IDs, academic notes, and scanned PDFs are never uploaded to any external server.

Key Features

  • Supports Both Scanned PDFs & Images: Upload multi-page PDF documents or standard image formats (.jpg, .png, .webp).
  • Multi-Language Recognition: Extract text accurately across English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Spanish, French, German, and Arabic.
  • Instant Copy & Export: Edit the recognized text right inside the workspace, copy it to your clipboard with one click, or download it as a .txt file.

How to Extract Text Using OCR

Drop your scanned PDF or image into the upload box above, pick the matching document language, and click Run OCR Extraction. Once complete, copy the extracted text or save it as a plain text file.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I extract text from a multi-page scanned PDF?

Yes! When you upload a PDF, the tool renders each page at high resolution in your browser and runs OCR page-by-page, clearly labeling each page in the extracted text output.

2. Why does the first scan take a few extra seconds?

On your first run for a selected language, your browser downloads the compact OCR language training model directly from Cloudflare's CDN and caches it locally for faster subsequent scans.

3. Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. All PDF rendering and OCR text recognition happen 100% locally in your browser with zero file uploads.
