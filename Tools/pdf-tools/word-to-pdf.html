Yes. All processing happens 100% client-side inside your browser memory using Mammoth.js and pdfmake. Your documents are never uploaded to any external server.

Can I convert Word files containing embedded images or screenshots?

Yes! The converter automatically extracts embedded images and screenshots from your .docx file and formats them into the output PDF.
