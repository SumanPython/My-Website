Why Convert MOV to MP4?

Apple devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac QuickTime) save recordings in the .mov container format. While ideal for Apple devices, MOV files often fail to play on Windows PCs, Android smartphones, or web upload forms. This tool converts your video into standard H.264 MP4 directly in your browser's memory—meaning your private videos never leave your device.
